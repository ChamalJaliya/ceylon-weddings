Status: confirmed (Sri Lanka first, two-sided, local + diaspora + destination)

These nine modules are in v1. Everything else from the core-modules research stays v1.5 / v2.

| # | Module | Surface | Why it is in MVP |

| 4 | Budget | Couple | LKR, multi-payer, plate-rate line items |

| 6 | Vendor listing | Vendor + Admin | Claim/create storefront |
